04 — QUESTIONS

Asked often

Straight answers.

What exactly is deleted when I quit?

Everything websites stored: HTTP cache, cookies, DOM storage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches. They live in RAM under an in-memory session and are wiped on exit. Your own settings, bookmarks and history database stay — unless you switch those off too.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Free forever, no accounts, no telemetry to monetize. There is no catch — there's also no company infrastructure phoning home.

Why does it say Beta?

Because it is. Kastrava (originally AnterSurf) is under active development — features land fast and rough edges exist. The privacy guarantees above are already enforced.

Which platforms are supported?

Linux (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, Fedora) and Windows 10+. macOS and Android builds are discontinued.

What search engine does it use?

KastravaSearch (kastravasearch.pp.ua) — Kastrava's own search — is the default. DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google and Ecosia are one click away in Settings.

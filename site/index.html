01 — UNDER THE HOOD

Six mechanisms. No marketing.

Every claim below is enforced in code, verifiable in seconds.

01

Session isolation

Cache, cookies, DOM storage and IndexedDB live in a RAM-only per-session sandbox and are wiped when you quit; crash leftovers are purged at the next launch. Your settings, bookmarks and history stay on disk until you clear them.

02

Tracking radar

Every third-party request and cookie is counted per tab, with a live score. Strict blocking, DNT headers, no referrers.

03

KastravaSearch by default

Kastrava's own search at kastravasearch.pp.ua. Switchable to DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google or Ecosia.

04

Private-by-default downloads

Click a download and it streams into a session-only area with pause, resume and progress tracking. Files stay locked to that browsing session and are wiped when you quit — save anything you want to keep to your Downloads folder.

05

Real keyboard control

Ctrl+T/W/Tab, Ctrl+Shift+T, F12 DevTools — OS-level shortcuts that work even with a page focused.

06

Guided first-run setup

Nine-step welcome: engine, theme, tracking, startup. Beta-labeled, honestly unfinished where it is.

06 — QUESTIONS

Asked often

Straight answers.

What exactly is deleted when I quit?

Everything websites stored: HTTP cache, cookies, DOM storage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches. They live in a private per-session sandbox and are wiped on every exit — even crash leftovers are purged at the next launch. Downloads follow the same rule: they stay locked to the session and are wiped unless you explicitly save them to your Downloads folder. Your own settings, bookmarks and history database stay — unless you switch those off too.

Is the core really free? What's the catch?

Free forever, no accounts, no telemetry to monetize. Premium ($2.59 / 34 days, no auto-renew) is a one-time payment per cycle that powers development — but everything below is free: session isolation (website data never touches disk), blockers, tracking protection, downloads, default keyboard shortcuts, KastravaSearch + DuckDuckGo, themes 1 light + 1 dark.

What does Premium actually unlock?

Tracking Radar (live per-tab privacy score), the full Cursor FX suite (all 8 effects + custom arrow), Workspaces, Split View, all 20 themes, extra search engines (Brave, Google, Ecosia), Reader Mode, Picture-in-Picture, Screenshot, the Localhost Dashboard, bookmark import/export, vertical tabs, and custom shortcut remapping. One license, one machine. No auto-renew — the app reminds you when it's time to renew.

Why is it bound to one machine?

Each license key is cryptographically signed to the machine code of the PC that activates it. That's the whole "one copy, one owner" idea — you can't share one purchase across machines. Hardware changes break it on purpose. Same-PC Linux reinstalls keep working.

I changed PCs / lost my key. What now?

Email planetofoses989@gmail.com with your order payment ID. We verify and re-issue / unbind your key. We'd rather re-key you than argue.

Does activation phone home my browsing data?

No. Activation sends only a one-way hash of your hardware identifiers (a machine code) plus your license key — once. No history, no pages, no keystrokes, nothing you browse ever leaves the machine. And once activated, the app works even fully offline.

Why does it say Beta?

Because it is. Kastrava (originally AnterSurf) is under active development — features land fast and rough edges exist. The privacy guarantees above are already enforced.

Which platforms are supported?

Linux (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, AppImage, Fedora/RHEL rpm), Windows (installer + portable, plus the Microsoft Store build) and Android (direct APK sideload; Play Store listing in progress). macOS builds are discontinued.

What search engine does it use?

KastravaSearch (kastravasearch.pp.ua) — Kastrava's own search — is the default. DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google and Ecosia are one click away in Settings.
